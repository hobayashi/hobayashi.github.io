Fighting Board for Android

About Fighting Board for Android

Fighting Board for Android is a score board app for fighting games. It is built using C#, Xamarin.Android, and Rx, providing a seamless experience for gamers.


Privacy Policy

Fighting Board for Android does not collect, store, or share any personal data. The app is designed to function entirely offline and does not require any user information to operate.
